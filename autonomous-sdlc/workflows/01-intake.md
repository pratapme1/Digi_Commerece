# 01 Intake

## Goal
Turn a raw request into a scoped work item with the right risk level.

## Steps
1. Classify the request: bug, feature, spike, refactor, release, or documentation.
2. Identify who is affected, what changes, and what must not change.
3. Create a numbered feature workspace with `new_feature.sh`.
4. Record assumptions, missing information, and blocking questions in `spec.md`.

## Outputs
- feature folder
- first-pass `spec.md`

## Exit Criteria
- scope is named
- owner role is clear
- risk level is captured
